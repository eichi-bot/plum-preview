COFFEE IMPORT

Coffee from across Indonesia.

01

Sourcing nationwide

Robusta, Arabica (including Gayo), Excelsa and Liberica from Sumatra and West, Central and East Java, and other regions on request.

02

Quality at the farm

At our partner growing area in Wonosalam, East Java, the coffee cooperative has introduced the Toyota Production System (TPS) through an initiative by Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS), a national university, and Toyota Motor Manufacturing Indonesia (TMMIN).

03

Export handled in one place

Our partners manage the official export procedures end to end. In Japan, we import jointly with Takihyo Co., Ltd., a Nagoya company founded in 1751, which handles import and sales.

04

Seen with our own eyes

Our CEO, Tomoko Ando, visits the farms in person and builds face-to-face relationships with the growers.
